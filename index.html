01 / About

Advocacy begins with listening.

I believe the people most affected by public systems should have a meaningful place in shaping them.

My interests centre on youth rights, child welfare, human rights, and the role that law and public policy can play in improving young people's experiences with institutions.

Through youth advocacy, collaboration, and policy-focused work, I aim to bring young people's perspectives into conversations where they are too often missing.

I am also interested in the legal profession and the ways thoughtful, principled legal work can help protect rights and strengthen access to justice.
