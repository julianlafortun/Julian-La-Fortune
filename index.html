01 / Mission & Mandate

Closing the Structural Gaps in Youth Protection.

Advocacy anchored in lived accountability, human rights law, and systemic reform across Ontario and Canada.

Statement of Purpose DCI Canada — Youth Advocate
“My mission is to ensure that no child fleeing an unsafe home situation falls through the structural gaps of our youth protection and crisis care frameworks.”

As a Youth Advocate for Defence for Children International (DCI) Canada, Julian La Fortune’s work is driven by direct lived experience navigating child welfare and crisis care systems as a survivor of psychological and emotional abuse.

Dedicated to reforming Ontario’s youth protection frameworks, his advocacy bridges front-line youth reality with institutional policy — pushing for stronger safeguards against violence toward children, accessible youth mental health interventions, and enforceable rights under international and domestic child protection standards.
