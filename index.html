CORE MISSION STATEMENT 01A
“Driven by lived experiences navigating child welfare and crisis care systems as a survivor of psychological and emotional abuse, dedicated to reforming Ontario’s youth protection frameworks so that no child fleeing an unsafe home situation falls through the structural cracks of care.”

Working as a Youth Advocate and Human Rights & Child Advocate, Julian translates firsthand knowledge of child welfare and crisis care systems into systemic advocacy focused on safety, accountability, and young people’s rights.

Alongside direct advocacy work in Ontario and Canada, he engages with international child care communities and continues studying law with a focus on protecting vulnerable youth.
