EDITORIAL MANIFESTO 01 // OVERVIEW
“No child fleeing an unsafe home situation should fall through the structural gaps of our care systems.”

Driven by my own lived experiences navigating the child welfare and crisis care systems as a survivor of psychological and emotional abuse, I have dedicated myself to reforming Ontario’s youth protection frameworks. My mission is to ensure that no child fleeing an unsafe home situation falls through the structural gaps of our care systems.

From a Canadian youth and child rights perspective, reforming care requires closing procedural blind spots, listening directly to young people in crisis, and aligning statutory and institutional safeguards with children’s human rights.
